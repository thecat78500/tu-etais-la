## Prompt de création de Lune

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: individual portrait game illustration for the French horror deduction game Tu Étais Là, displayed as one of five equal door images on an iPad. Create ONE full closed antique wooden door, viewed perfectly straight on, including its entire rectangular frame and threshold, centered, vertical portrait composition. A believable abandoned French manor house interior: dark weathered walnut panels, tarnished brass handle, cracked plaster immediately surrounding the frame, subtle dust. One large unmistakable crescent MOON symbol physically carved and inlaid in aged pale brass in the upper central panel of the door, very readable at small size. Warm low candlelight from the side, muted charcoal, brown and old gold palette, cinematic atmospheric realism, detailed real wood, spooky without gore. The door itself and symbol must remain clearly readable, avoid crushing blacks. Closed door, no view beyond it, neutral ambiguous mood, no indication whether it is the winning or losing door. Frame occupies almost entire image with narrow consistent wall margins. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, people, candles in foreground, extra doors, illustrated borders, or UI. Produce a polished realistic game asset, not a mockup.
